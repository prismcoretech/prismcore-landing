Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who we are

PrismCore is run by PrismCore AI, a Texas limited liability company. This policy covers our website at prismcoretech.com and the information you share when you book a call with us.

What we collect

When you book a discovery call, Calendly collects your name, work email, company and anything you tell us about what's going on. This site has no contact forms and doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies. When you load a page, our hosting provider receives basic technical details, such as your IP address and browser type, which it needs in order to show you the page. Our home page also loads Calendly's booking tool, so Calendly receives the same details when that page loads.

How we use it

We use your booking details to prepare for and hold your call, send you the free resource we offer, and follow up about your inquiry. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Third parties

We use Calendly to book calls, and it handles your booking details under its own privacy policy (calendly.com/privacy). Calendly may set its own cookies when you open the booking window. Your booking details are also stored in our email and calendar. Like any website, ours runs on a hosting provider that processes basic technical details, such as your IP address, to deliver the page.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a client, we delete your booking details within 12 months of our last contact. If you do, we keep them for as long as we work together and as long as the law requires afterward.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local law, and we'll honor those requests too.

Contact

Email support@prismcoretech.com with questions about this policy or your data. If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
