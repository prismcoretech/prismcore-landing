Questions we usually get

How can we trust you with access to our systems?

You control our access to every system and can revoke it at any time. Before we start, we agree together on the minimum access the work needs, and you remove it as soon as the project is done or your retainer ends. It's all laid out in a clear contract, with an NDA as standard.

What if you break our logins?

Every rollout is staged. We test with test users and a small pilot group before it reaches everyone else, and a rollback plan is ready in the rare case something goes wrong.

Our IT team or MSP can handle this.

We'd rather work alongside them. Specialized identity and automation skills are rare and expensive to hire at your size, and that's the gap we fill. We're not here to replace anyone: we hand the systems over to your team or help maintain them, and either way they get real time back each week.

We already do it manually, and it works.

Manual works up to a point. Then the company grows, a hiring surge hits, or one termination gets missed.
